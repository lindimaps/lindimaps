Privacy Policy

LindiMaps Studio

Official Document

This document explains how LindiMaps ("we", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data in accordance with Law No. 9887, dated 10.03.2008 "On the Protection of Personal Data" in the Republic of Albania and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Identity of the Controller

The controller of your data is LindiMaps Studio, based in Tirana, Albania. We are responsible for ensuring that your data is processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently, guaranteeing the integrity of any geospatial information.

2. Data We Collect

While using our GIS (Geographic Information Systems) services and our web platform, we may process the following categories:

  • Identification data: First name, last name, and email address when you use our contact forms.
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, operating system, and navigation data to improve map performance.
  • Geospatial data: Requested coordinates or areas of interest, which are processed anonymously for statistical purposes.

3. Legal Basis and Purpose

Data processing is carried out to provide accurate cartographic services and to improve the user experience, based on:

  • Your consent: When you provide us with information voluntarily.
  • Contract: To fulfill our obligations to our corporate clients.
  • Legal obligation: To respond to requests from regulatory authorities in Albania.

4. Data Security

Security is our top priority, especially considering the sensitive nature of location data:

  • Total Encryption: All transfers are performed via HTTPS (SSL/TLS) protocol.
  • 24/7 Monitoring: Our systems are monitored for unauthorized access attempts.
  • Minimization: We only collect what is absolutely necessary for the operation of the service.

5. Your Rights

As a data subject, you have full rights that you can exercise at any time:

  • Access & Information: To know what data we hold about you.
  • Right to be Forgotten: To request the deletion of all your traces from our servers.
  • Portability: To receive your data in a structured format.

Questions or Legal Requests?

Our data protection office is at your disposal for any ambiguity or specific request regarding your privacy.
